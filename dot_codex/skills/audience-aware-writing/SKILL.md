# Audience-Aware Writing

Write from the reader's actual context, not the author's accumulated context.
Writing never grants publication, delegated identity, or outreach authority.

## Establish the reader and decision

Identify the reader, what they have actually seen, relevant expertise, and
the judgment or action the message must enable. Domain familiarity does not
imply knowledge of private decisions, unposted work, or prior conversations.

Name the concrete artifact, behavior, or problem before referring to it.
Introduce unseen prerequisites only when they affect the actual decision.
State what changed, why it matters, and what happens next; explain the
observable behavior before internal mechanics.

Answer the actual question in its original conversation. Treat quoted
context as already available, match the reader's understanding, and add the
new decision instead of restating known facts. Distinguish an accepted
concern from a declined mechanism without inventing agreement or certainty.

Attach one verified problem and ask to existing context; keep tracking in the
operational record. Once a human owner accepts responsibility, stop repeating
reviews, CI, approvals, confirmations, or mentions. Resume only for a
materially new decision, safety concern, or demonstrated CI-selection gap.

## Write and verify the result

Use concrete nouns, plain verbs, proportionate context, and readable
formatting. Remove praise, chronology, process narration, implementation
trivia, repetition, and unsupported consequences. Link directly to primary
evidence and format citations according to the destination; a link supports
an explanation rather than replacing it.

Keep every artifact and antecedent identifiable. Preserve the actual
speaker's authorship; never turn the assistant's work into someone else's
first-person claim. Represent partial feedback, uncertainty, disagreement,
and the requested decision accurately.

Respect the actual destination's publication, attribution, correction, and
explicitly authorized feedback-invitation policy through its owning delivery
workflow. Never silently change the meaning of published prose or make
existing responses appear to answer a different message.
